About Netsera

Senior network help without the oversized MSP experience.

Netsera exists for small and mid-sized businesses that need experienced network engineering but do not need a full internal network team.

The work is focused on the parts of IT infrastructure that often cause the most disruption when they are wrong: Wi-Fi, switching, routing, firewalls, VPNs, WAN, SD-WAN, remote access, and network security.

Built for real SMB network problems

Many SMB networks were built in layers: one firewall change here, one switch replacement there, a few access points added when coverage got bad, and a VPN setup that no one wants to touch.

Netsera helps make sense of that environment. We look at how the network is actually being used, what equipment is in place, what is failing, and what the business needs before recommending changes.

Nearly 20 years of network engineering experience

Netsera brings nearly 20 years of hands-on IT and network engineering experience to small and mid-sized business environments.

That experience includes enterprise wireless, switching, routing, firewalls, VPN, WAN, SD-WAN, remote access, NAC, DNS/IPAM, troubleshooting, multi-site design, and cutover support.

Vendor-neutral by design

Netsera works across mixed-vendor environments and does not force every business into one platform.

Experience includes Fortinet, Cisco, Meraki, UniFi/Ubiquiti, Aruba, WatchGuard, pfSense, Ruckus, SonicWall, HP networking, and mixed environments.

The goal is to recommend and support the network design that fits the business, budget, risk, and operational needs.

What to expect

  • A clear conversation about the issue
  • A practical recommendation, not a sales script
  • Careful implementation when changes are needed
  • Documentation and handoff notes where appropriate
  • Honest guidance when a request is not the right fit
  • Clear scope before work begins

Who Netsera helps

Netsera works with small and mid-sized businesses across the north metro Twin Cities and surrounding area, including offices, warehouses, professional services firms, multi-site businesses, internal IT teams, and MSPs that need senior network escalation help.
