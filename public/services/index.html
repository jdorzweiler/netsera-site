FAQ

Common questions

Do we have to replace all our network equipment?

Not necessarily. The first step is to understand what is failing and why. Sometimes the fix is configuration, placement, documentation, cleanup, or a smaller targeted replacement — not a full rebuild.

Can Netsera work with our current IT provider?

Yes. Netsera can support internal IT teams, MSPs, and business owners who need deeper network expertise for a specific issue, project, or escalation.

Does Netsera only work with one vendor?

No. Netsera is vendor-neutral and has experience across Fortinet, Cisco, Meraki, UniFi/Ubiquiti, WatchGuard, pfSense, Aruba, Ruckus, SonicWall, HP networking, and mixed-vendor environments.

Can Netsera help with one project instead of ongoing support?

Yes. Netsera is a good fit for scoped troubleshooting, wireless improvements, firewall/VPN work, switch refreshes, WAN changes, network reviews, and cutover support.
