Helpful details to include

You do not need to provide all of this, but these details can help Netsera understand the request faster:

  • What is slow, unreliable, failing, or being replaced
  • How many users, locations, or devices are affected
  • Whether this is a current issue or a planned project
  • Any known equipment or platforms involved, such as Fortinet, Cisco, Meraki, UniFi/Ubiquiti, Aruba, WatchGuard, pfSense, Ruckus, SonicWall, or HP networking
  • Any timing constraints, outage windows, or business hours to work around
